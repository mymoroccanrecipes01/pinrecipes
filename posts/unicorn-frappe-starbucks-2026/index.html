This homemade unicorn frappe is a fun and colorful twist on the Starbucks classic, blending pastel swirls with fluffy whipped cream and sprinkles. It’s perfect for brightening up any afternoon or special treat time. I love how easy it is to whip up at home with just a few simple ingredients!

Introduction

I first tried the Starbucks unicorn frappe a few years ago and instantly fell in love with its magical colors and sweet, creamy flavor. But buying it every time got expensive, so I started experimenting at home. After a few tries, I found a way to recreate that perfect pastel swirl and creamy texture using simple syrups and food coloring. What makes this version special is the layering of the syrups and the cool blend of ice cream and milk that gives it a smooth, indulgent finish. I also love topping it with whipped cream and rainbow sprinkles for that extra fun pop. It’s a treat that’s just as delightful to make as it is to sip, perfect for brightening up a summer afternoon or a special occasion. Plus, it’s easy enough for anyone to whip up without fancy equipment or hard-to-find ingredients. If you’ve been craving that colorful Starbucks magic but want to save some money (and customize it your way), this recipe is for you!

What You'll Need

Makes 2 servings

  • 1 cup ice cubes
  • 1/2 cup milk (whole or your choice)
  • 1/4 cup vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tablespoons white chocolate syrup
  • 1 tablespoon raspberry syrup
  • 1 tablespoon blue curaçao syrup or blue raspberry syrup
  • 1/4 teaspoon edible pink and blue food coloring
  • 1/2 cup whipped cream
  • 1 tablespoon rainbow sprinkles

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by blending the ice cubes, milk, vanilla ice cream, and white chocolate syrup in a high-speed blender until smooth and creamy. This combination creates the frappe’s rich base with a perfect icy texture. Avoid over-blending as you want some thickness but not a slushy mess. The ice cream adds creaminess while the white chocolate syrup gives a subtle sweetness and silky mouthfeel. Blend for about 30 seconds until you see no large ice chunks and the mixture is velvety smooth.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Smooth, thick frappe without large ice pieces, creamy texture visible
Step 2
Divide the frappe base into two clear cups, filling about half of each. Next, gently drizzle raspberry syrup on one side and blue curaçao or blue raspberry syrup on the other side of each cup. Use a small spoon or squeeze bottle to create swirls without mixing completely. Add a tiny drop of pink and blue food coloring on top of the syrups to intensify the pastel effect. This layering is what creates the iconic unicorn look. Be careful not to stir too much or the colors will blend into a dull purple.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Distinct pink and blue swirls visible through the cup, pastel colors bright and separated
Step 3
Top each frappe generously with fluffy whipped cream. The whipped cream adds a rich, airy contrast to the cold frappe and helps hold the sprinkles in place. Use a spoon or piping bag for a pretty swirl. Then sprinkle rainbow sprinkles evenly over the whipped cream for that fun, magical finish. The sprinkles add a bit of crunch and vibrant color that makes this drink special.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Whipped cream peaks hold shape well, sprinkles evenly distributed and colorful
Step 4
Serve immediately with a wide straw to enjoy the layers and creamy whipped topping together. If you want to keep the layers distinct longer, serve without stirring. The frappe is best enjoyed cold and fresh as the ice will slowly melt and the colors will blend over time.
⏱ Time: Serve immediately
✓ Doneness: Cold drink with visible layers and fresh whipped cream
Step 5
To customize, try swapping the syrups for your favorite flavors or add a splash of vanilla extract to the base for extra depth. You can also make it dairy-free by using coconut or almond milk and vegan whipped cream. Just keep the layering technique for the best visual effect.
⏱ Time: Optional customization
✓ Doneness: Flavor adjusted to preference without losing the colorful swirl effect
Step 6
If preparing ahead, blend the frappe base and refrigerate it separately. Add syrups, whipped cream, and sprinkles just before serving to keep the colors vibrant and the whipped cream fresh. This helps maintain the texture and visual appeal.
⏱ Time: Prep ahead tip
✓ Doneness: Fresh whipped cream and distinct layers on serving
Step 7
For a thicker frappe, add more ice cream or reduce the milk slightly. For a lighter version, increase milk and reduce ice cream. Adjust based on your texture preference but keep the balance so the drink isn’t too watery or overly thick.
⏱ Time: Adjust texture
✓ Doneness: Desired frappe thickness achieved without separation
Step 8
Use good quality syrups or make your own simple syrup infused with natural fruit juices for a fresher taste. Artificial syrups can sometimes overpower the delicate flavors and colors.
⏱ Time: Syrup quality tip
✓ Doneness: Balanced sweetness and natural flavor shine through
Step 9
Clean your blender immediately after use to avoid sticky residue buildup from syrups and ice cream. This helps maintain your equipment and ensures smooth blending every time.
⏱ Time: Cleanup tip
✓ Doneness: No sticky residue left in blender, ready for next use

Pro Tips

Use a clear cup to showcase the beautiful pastel swirls for the full unicorn effect.
The visual appeal is a huge part of what makes the unicorn frappe special. A clear cup lets the layered colors shine through and makes the drink more enticing. Using opaque cups hides this effect and diminishes the fun presentation.
Add syrups slowly and on opposite sides to prevent colors from blending too much.
Pouring the syrups carefully keeps the pink and blue layers distinct, preserving the iconic swirl look. If poured too quickly or mixed, the colors combine into a muddy purple, losing the magical appearance.
Use cold ingredients and blend quickly to keep the frappe icy and refreshing.
Warm or room temperature ingredients cause the ice to melt prematurely, resulting in a watery drink. Keeping everything cold maintains the frappe's texture and ensures the colors stay vibrant without dilution.
Top with whipped cream just before serving to keep it fluffy and fresh.
Whipped cream melts quickly on cold drinks. Adding it last preserves its texture and appearance, making the drink look inviting and taste indulgent.
Experiment with natural fruit syrups for a fresher, less artificial flavor.
Many commercial syrups contain artificial flavors and colors that can taste harsh or chemical. Homemade or natural syrups enhance the flavor complexity and improve the overall quality of the drink.
Serve with a wide straw to enjoy the layers and whipped cream together.
A wide straw lets you sip the creamy whipped topping and the icy frappe layers simultaneously, delivering the full flavor experience in every mouthful.
Clean your blender immediately after use to avoid sticky residue buildup.
Syrups and ice cream can leave sticky residues that harden if left too long. Cleaning right away keeps your blender in good condition and ready for your next recipe.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Over-blending the frappe base until too thin

Why it happens People often blend too long trying to get a smooth texture, but this melts the ice and thins the drink excessively.

Fix Blend just until the ice is crushed and the mixture is creamy but still thick. Stop blending as soon as no large ice pieces remain to keep the frappe icy and refreshing.

Mixing the syrup layers too much

Why it happens In a rush, some stir the syrups into the base, which causes the colors to blend into an unappealing muddy shade.

Fix Drizzle syrups gently on opposite sides and avoid stirring. This keeps the colors separated and vibrant for the signature unicorn swirl effect.

Adding whipped cream too early

Why it happens Whipped cream melts quickly on cold drinks, especially if the frappe sits too long before serving.

Fix Add the whipped cream topping just before serving to maintain its fluffy texture and appearance.

Using warm milk or ice cream

Why it happens Not chilling ingredients causes the ice to melt quickly, resulting in a watery frappe and dull colors.

Fix Use cold milk and ice cream straight from the fridge or freezer to keep the frappe chilled and thick.

Using low-quality or artificial syrups

Why it happens Some syrups have overpowering artificial flavors or colors that affect taste and appearance negatively.

Fix Choose natural or homemade syrups with real fruit juice when possible for a fresher, cleaner flavor and better color.

Variations

Dairy-Free Unicorn Frappe

Swap out regular milk and ice cream for almond or coconut milk and vegan ice cream. Use coconut whipped cream on top. The flavor stays vibrant and creamy but is suitable for dairy-free diets.

Note: Dairy-free ingredients may produce a slightly thinner texture; consider adding more ice cream or ice to thicken.

Chocolate Unicorn Frappe

Add a tablespoon of chocolate syrup to the base blend for a mocha twist. Layer with raspberry and blue syrups as usual. The chocolate adds richness and a new flavor dimension.

Note: Chocolate syrup can darken the base color; use sparingly to keep pastel tones visible.

Extra Fruity Unicorn Frappe

Add fresh raspberries or blueberries into the blender for a natural fruit burst. This enhances flavor and adds texture with real fruit pieces.

Note: Blend fruit gently to avoid over-thickening; adjust ice quantity accordingly.

Sugar-Free Unicorn Frappe

Use sugar-free syrups and a low-sugar ice cream alternative. This keeps the frappe sweet and colorful without added sugar, perfect for low-carb diets.

Note: Sugar-free versions may have less intense colors and sweetness; adjust syrups to taste.

Unicorn Frappe Float

Top the frappe with a scoop of vanilla or rainbow sherbet instead of whipped cream for a fun float variation. The sherbet melts into the drink for extra creaminess.

Note: Serve immediately to enjoy the float before it melts completely.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

The unicorn frappe is best served immediately and should not be left at room temperature for more than 30 minutes, as the ice will melt and the whipped cream will lose its texture. Cover with a lid if needed to prevent contamination.

Refrigerator

You can store the frappe base (without whipped cream or syrups) in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours. The color and texture may change slightly. Whipped cream and sprinkles should be added fresh before serving.

Freezer

Freeze the frappe base in a sealed container for up to 1 month. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator and stir before serving. Do not freeze whipped cream or sprinkles as they will lose texture.

Reheating

This frappe is a cold beverage and not intended to be reheated. If the mixture separates after storage, blend again briefly to restore texture.

Make Ahead

Prepare the frappe base and syrups a day ahead and keep refrigerated. Assemble with whipped cream and sprinkles just before serving to maintain freshness and appearance.

FAQ

Can I make the unicorn frappe without food coloring?
Yes, you can omit the food coloring, but the vibrant pastel swirls will be less pronounced. The syrups provide some color, but the overall visual effect will be subtler. Using natural fruit purees can add gentle color without artificial dyes.
Can I use frozen fruit instead of syrups?
Frozen fruit can add natural flavor and color but won’t create the same layered syrup effect. Blend fruit into the base for a fruity frappe, but you’ll lose the distinct unicorn swirl look.
