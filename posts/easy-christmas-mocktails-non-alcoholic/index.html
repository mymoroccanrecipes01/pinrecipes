These easy Christmas mocktails non alcoholic are perfect for adding festive cheer without the booze. Made with fresh cranberries, pomegranate, and sparkling water, they’re refreshing and kid-friendly. Great for holiday parties or cozy nights in!

Introduction

I first made these easy Christmas mocktails non alcoholic a few years ago when my family wanted festive drinks but no one was drinking alcohol. I wanted something bright, flavorful, and fun for everyone, especially the kids. This recipe uses fresh cranberry and pomegranate juices for that perfect balance of tart and sweet, plus sparkling water for fizz. I love adding cinnamon sticks and rosemary sprigs to make it feel truly festive and cozy. It’s so simple to put together, no fancy equipment or hard-to-find ingredients. The trick I learned early on is to chill everything well and add the sparkling water last to keep the bubbles lively. These mocktails look beautiful in clear glasses with a sugared rim, making them party-ready in minutes. Whether you’re hosting a big holiday gathering or just want a special drink for a quiet night in, these mocktails are a hit. They’re refreshing, colorful, and bring a little sparkle to any Christmas celebration. Plus, they’re kid-friendly and easy enough for beginners to mix up without fuss. I think you’ll love how they brighten up your holiday table and get everyone in the festive spirit!

What You'll Need

Approximately 6 servings

  • 2 cups cranberry juice, chilled
  • 1 cup pomegranate juice, chilled
  • 2 cups sparkling water or club soda, chilled
  • 1/2 cup fresh pomegranate seeds
  • 1/2 cup fresh cranberries
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • Fresh rosemary sprigs for garnish
  • Powdered sugar for rimming glasses (optional)
  • Ice cubes

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by chilling your cranberry juice, pomegranate juice, and sparkling water in the fridge for at least an hour before making the mocktails. This is crucial because it keeps the drinks refreshing without diluting the flavors with too much ice. When you pour cold liquids, the sparkling water retains its fizz better, giving your mocktail that lively sparkle everyone loves. Avoid adding ice too early as it melts and waters down the drink. Instead, add ice just before serving to keep the flavors vibrant and crisp.
⏱ Time: 60 minutes chilling
✓ Doneness: All liquids are cold to the touch and ready for mixing
Step 2
Prepare your glasses by rimming them with powdered sugar if desired. To do this, rub a lemon wedge around the rim of each glass then dip it into a plate of powdered sugar. This adds a festive frosted look and a touch of sweetness with every sip. Next, add a few fresh cranberries and pomegranate seeds into each glass along with a cinnamon stick. These garnishes not only look beautiful but infuse subtle warm spice as you sip. Be gentle when adding seeds so they don’t sink immediately, keeping the presentation appealing.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Glasses have a frosted sugar rim and colorful garnishes neatly arranged
Step 3
In a large pitcher, combine the chilled cranberry and pomegranate juices. Stir gently to blend the flavors without disturbing the natural juices too much. Pour this juice mixture evenly into the prepared glasses, filling them about three-quarters full. This ensures enough room for the sparkling water and ice without overflowing. The deep red hues create a festive base that looks gorgeous in clear glassware.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Juices are well mixed and glasses are filled to three-quarters
Step 4
Slowly top off each glass with chilled sparkling water or club soda. Pour gently over the back of a spoon or down the side of the glass to maintain the bubbles and prevent the drink from foaming over. This step adds the signature fizz to your mocktail, making it feel special and celebratory. Stir lightly just once to combine, being careful not to lose the carbonation.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Bubbles are visible and the drink is gently mixed without foam spilling
Step 5
Add ice cubes to each glass just before serving to keep the drink cold without diluting the flavors too soon. The ice also adds an inviting chill that makes these mocktails perfect for any holiday party or cozy evening. If you’re serving outdoors or in a warm room, consider using large ice cubes or reusable ice stones to slow melting.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Glasses are chilled with ice cubes floating on top
Step 6
Garnish each glass with a fresh rosemary sprig tucked alongside the cinnamon stick. The rosemary adds a lovely piney aroma that complements the tart fruit juices and warm spice. It also enhances the festive look and makes each drink feel thoughtfully crafted. Plus, rosemary is easy to find and adds a touch of elegance without extra effort.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Garnishes are fresh, green, and neatly arranged in each glass
Step 7
Serve your Christmas mocktails immediately to enjoy the full sparkle and fresh flavors. If you need to prepare ahead, keep the juice mixture chilled separately and add sparkling water and garnishes just before serving. This keeps the fizz intact and the presentation fresh. Remember, the key to a great mocktail is serving it cold and bubbly!
⏱ Time: Immediate serving
✓ Doneness: Drinks are bright, fizzy, and visually appealing
Step 8
For an extra festive touch, try adding a splash of fresh lime juice or a few drops of vanilla extract to the juice mixture before pouring. This adds brightness and complexity without overpowering the natural fruit flavors. Taste test as you go to keep the balance just right.
⏱ Time: Optional extra 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Balanced flavor with a subtle extra note enhancing the drink
Step 9
Clean up is easy with this recipe since there’s no cooking involved. Simply rinse out your pitcher and glasses right after use to prevent any sticky residue from the fruit juices. This keeps your kitchen ready for the next holiday gathering without hassle.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: All utensils and glasses are clean and ready for storage

Pro Tips

Always chill your juices and sparkling water before mixing.
Cold liquids help preserve carbonation and keep the drink refreshing without needing excessive ice, which dilutes flavor. Serving chilled also enhances the crisp, bright taste that makes mocktails enjoyable.
Pour sparkling water gently over the back of a spoon.
This technique minimizes agitation and preserves bubbles, ensuring your mocktail stays fizzy longer. Agitating sparkling water too much causes it to lose carbonation quickly, resulting in a flat drink.
Use fresh garnishes like rosemary and cinnamon sticks.
Fresh herbs and whole spices add aroma and visual appeal, elevating the drinking experience. They also slowly infuse subtle flavors, making each sip more complex and enjoyable.
Rim glasses with powdered sugar for a festive touch.
The sugar adds a sweet contrast to the tart juices and creates an inviting frosted look that enhances presentation. It also melts slowly, balancing flavors as you sip.
Add ice cubes just before serving.
Ice chills the drink without diluting it prematurely. Adding ice too early causes melting and watering down, which dulls the vibrant flavors and fizz.
Try large ice cubes or reusable ice stones for longer chill.
Larger ice melts slower, keeping your mocktail cold longer without watering it down quickly. Reusable stones are great for preserving flavor and avoiding dilution.
Experiment with a splash of fresh lime juice or vanilla extract.
These additions brighten and add complexity to the flavor profile, making the mocktail more interesting without overpowering the natural fruit juices.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using warm or room temperature juices and sparkling water.

Why it happens Sometimes people rush preparation and don’t chill ingredients properly, thinking it won’t affect the drink much.

Fix Always chill all liquids thoroughly before mixing. This preserves carbonation and keeps the mocktail refreshing. If you forget, add ice just before serving but expect some dilution.

Pouring sparkling water too quickly causing loss of fizz.

Why it happens Pouring directly into the glass without care agitates the bubbles, causing them to dissipate quickly.

Fix Pour sparkling water gently over the back of a spoon or down the side of the glass to preserve carbonation and keep the drink lively.

Adding ice too early.

Why it happens Adding ice at the start causes it to melt and water down the drink before serving.

Fix Add ice cubes only just before serving to maintain flavor integrity and chill the drink effectively.

Using sweetened or cocktail blend juices.

Why it happens Pre-sweetened juices can throw off the balance, making the mocktail overly sweet or artificial tasting.

Fix Use 100% pure unsweetened juices to control sweetness and achieve a natural tart flavor.

Skipping garnishes or using dried herbs.

Why it happens Sometimes garnishes are overlooked or dried herbs are used for convenience.

Fix Use fresh rosemary sprigs and whole cinnamon sticks to add aroma, flavor, and festive presentation.

Variations

Citrus Christmas Mocktail

Swap half the cranberry juice for fresh orange juice and add thin orange slices as garnish. This adds bright citrus notes and a sweeter profile while keeping the festive red color. Expect a slightly smoother, fruitier drink.

Note: Fresh orange juice is less acidic, so balance sweetness carefully to avoid overpowering the tartness.

Spiced Apple Christmas Mocktail

Replace pomegranate juice with apple cider and add a few whole cloves to the cinnamon sticks for extra warmth. This variation is perfect for cozy winter evenings with a comforting apple-spice flavor.

Note: Whole cloves infuse slowly; remove before serving to avoid bitterness.

Ginger Lime Christmas Mocktail

Add fresh grated ginger and a splash of lime juice to the juice mixture for a zesty twist. Garnish with a lime wheel and candied ginger for a spicy, refreshing holiday drink.

Note: Ginger adds a spicy kick; adjust quantity to taste to avoid overpowering the fruit flavors.

Berry Festive Mocktail

Mix in a handful of fresh raspberries and blueberries with the pomegranate and cranberry juices. This adds vibrant color and a sweet-tart berry flavor that’s perfect for holiday gatherings.

Note: Fresh berries can be muddled slightly for more flavor or left whole for visual appeal.

Vanilla Cinnamon Christmas Mocktail

Add a few drops of pure vanilla extract to the juice blend and increase the cinnamon sticks for a warm, cozy flavor profile. This mocktail feels indulgent yet remains alcohol-free and easy to make.

Note: Use pure vanilla extract sparingly to avoid overpowering the other flavors.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

These mocktails should not be kept at room temperature for more than 2 hours as the sparkling water will lose its fizz and the juices may ferment slightly. Cover glasses with plastic wrap or lids if needed temporarily.

Refrigerator

Store the juice mixture in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Add sparkling water and garnishes just before serving to keep the fizz and freshness. Prepared mocktails are best served immediately and do not store well once mixed.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as it will alter the texture and flavor of the juices and remove carbonation from sparkling water. Instead, freeze leftover juice portions separately without sparkling water.

Reheating

Reheating is not applicable for this cold beverage. Serve chilled for best flavor and fizz.

Make Ahead

Prepare and chill the juice mixture 1-2 days ahead and keep refrigerated. On the day of serving, add sparkling water, ice, and garnishes just before guests arrive to ensure maximum freshness and bubbles.

FAQ

Can I use bottled cranberry juice cocktail instead of pure cranberry juice?
Bottled cranberry juice cocktails often contain added sugars and other juices, which can make your mocktail overly sweet or change the flavor balance. For the best tart and natural taste, I recommend using 100% pure cranberry juice without added sweeteners.
How do I keep the sparkling water fizzy when mixing the mocktail?
To keep the bubbles intact, always pour sparkling water gently over the back of a spoon or down the side of the glass. Also, chill the sparkling water beforehand and add it last, just before serving. Avoid stirring vigorously to prevent carbonation loss.
Can I make this mocktail ahead of time?
You can prepare and chill the juice mixture 1-2 days ahead, but add sparkling water, ice, and garnishes only just before serving to maintain fizz and freshness. Once mixed with sparkling water, the drink is best consumed immediately.
What can I use if I don’t have fresh rosemary or cinnamon sticks?
Fresh rosemary and cinnamon sticks add aroma and a festive look, but if unavailable, you can skip them or use a small sprinkle of ground cinnamon and a sprig of fresh mint as alternatives. The flavor will be slightly different but still enjoyable.
Is there a way to make this mocktail less tart?
If you find the drink too tart, try adding a small amount of simple syrup or honey to taste. Alternatively, incorporate a splash of orange juice to add natural sweetness and balance the acidity.
Can I substitute sparkling water with soda or tonic water?
You can use soda water or tonic water, but tonic has a distinct bitter flavor that may alter the mocktail’s taste. Plain sparkling water or club soda is best for a neutral, refreshing fizz.
Are there any non-alcoholic alternatives to cinnamon sticks for garnish?
If you don’t have cinnamon sticks, consider using star anise or a twist of orange peel for a different aromatic garnish. These add visual appeal and unique flavor notes.
How many servings does this recipe make?
This recipe yields approximately 6 servings, depending on glass size. It’s easy to scale up or down based on your party size.
Can I use frozen cranberries or pomegranate seeds?
Yes, frozen fruits work fine as garnishes or added to the juice mixture, but fresh is preferred for best texture and appearance. Thaw frozen fruits before use to avoid excess water diluting the drink.
What’s the best way to serve these mocktails at a holiday party?
Serve the juice mixture chilled in a large pitcher and provide sparkling water, ice, and garnishes separately. Let guests mix their own drinks for a fun interactive experience and to keep fizz fresh.
