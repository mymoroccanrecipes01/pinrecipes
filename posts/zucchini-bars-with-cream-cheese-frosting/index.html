Easy Zucchini Bars with Cream Cheese Frosting for a Moist, Delicious Treat

These zucchini bars are a moist, lightly spiced treat topped with creamy, tangy cream cheese frosting. I love how the zucchini keeps them soft and fresh. Perfect for afternoon snacks or casual get-togethers!

Introduction

I first made these zucchini bars years ago when I was looking for a way to use up an abundance of zucchini from my garden. I’ve tried a few recipes, but this one quickly became my go-to because it’s so moist and tender without being overly sweet. What really sets it apart is the cream cheese frosting — it adds just the right tang and creaminess that makes these bars feel like a special treat, not just a vegetable dessert. The trick I learned is to squeeze out as much moisture as possible from the shredded zucchini before mixing it in. That keeps the bars from getting soggy but still keeps them soft and tender. Plus, I like to use a mix of granulated and brown sugar for a subtle caramel flavor that works beautifully with the cinnamon and vanilla. When these bars come out of the oven, they’re golden and lightly spiced, and once topped with the frosting, they’re downright irresistible. Perfect for afternoon snacks, potlucks, or just whenever you want a cozy, homemade dessert that feels a little fancy but is so easy to make.

What You'll Need

Approximately 8 large bars, 8 servings

  • 2 cups shredded zucchini, squeezed dry
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • For the frosting:
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by shredding your zucchini and then place it in a clean kitchen towel or cheesecloth. Squeeze firmly to remove as much moisture as possible. This step is crucial because zucchini holds a lot of water, and if you don’t drain it well, your bars will turn out soggy instead of moist and tender. The goal is to have shredded zucchini that’s damp but not dripping. Set aside.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Zucchini should feel damp but no excess water should drip when squeezed.
Step 2
In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt. Mixing the dry ingredients first ensures even distribution of the leavening agents and spices, which helps your bars rise properly and have balanced flavor. If you skip this step, you might get uneven pockets of baking soda or clumps of cinnamon.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Dry ingredients are well combined with no visible clumps.
Step 3
In a separate bowl, beat the eggs, granulated sugar, brown sugar, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract until smooth and well combined. This wet mixture provides moisture and sweetness while the oil keeps the bars tender. Beating the eggs well helps incorporate air, contributing to a lighter texture.
⏱ Time: 3
✓ Doneness: Wet ingredients are smooth and slightly thickened.
Step 4
Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, stirring gently to combine. Don’t overmix here; just fold until no streaks of flour remain. Overmixing can develop gluten and make the bars tough instead of tender. The batter will be thick but moist.
⏱ Time: 3
✓ Doneness: Batter is uniform in color and texture without dry patches.
Step 5
Fold the drained shredded zucchini into the batter until evenly distributed. This adds moisture and subtle texture to the bars. Make sure the zucchini is evenly mixed for consistent moisture and flavor throughout.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Zucchini is evenly dispersed, batter looks thick and moist.
Step 6
Pour the batter into a greased or parchment-lined 9x13-inch baking pan, spreading it evenly. An even layer ensures uniform baking and prevents thinner edges from drying out. Use a spatula to smooth the top gently.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Batter surface is flat and smooth without gaps.
Step 7
Bake in a preheated 350°F (175°C) oven for 25-30 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Avoid opening the oven door too often to keep the temperature steady. Baking too long dries the bars, too short leaves them undercooked.
⏱ Time: 30
✓ Doneness: Golden brown edges with a toothpick test showing no wet batter.
Step 8
While the bars cool, prepare the cream cheese frosting by beating softened cream cheese and butter together until smooth and creamy. Gradually add powdered sugar and vanilla extract, beating until fluffy. This frosting adds a tangy richness that balances the sweet, spiced bars perfectly.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Frosting is smooth, thick, and spreadable without lumps.
Step 9
Once the bars have cooled completely, spread the cream cheese frosting evenly over the top. Cooling is important so the frosting doesn’t melt and slide off. Cut into squares and serve. These bars keep well and taste even better the next day!
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Frosting is set and bars hold their shape when cut.

Pro Tips

Squeeze zucchini thoroughly before adding to the batter.
Removing excess water prevents soggy bars by controlling moisture content. If too wet, the batter won’t set properly and will bake into a dense, gummy texture. Proper moisture balance is key to tender, moist bars.
Don’t overmix the batter once the dry ingredients are added.
Overmixing develops gluten, which makes baked goods tough and chewy. Gently folding until just combined keeps the crumb tender and light, preserving the soft texture zucchini adds.
Let the bars cool completely before frosting.
Applying frosting to warm bars causes it to melt and slide off, ruining the appearance and texture. Cooling ensures the frosting sets properly and the bars hold their shape when sliced.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Not draining zucchini enough

Why it happens Many assume shredded zucchini can be added directly, but its high water content can make the bars soggy.

Fix Use a clean towel or cheesecloth to squeeze out as much liquid as possible before mixing. This keeps the bars moist but structured.

Overbaking the bars

Why it happens Leaving bars in the oven too long dries them out, especially since zucchini adds moisture that can mask doneness.

Fix Start checking at 25 minutes with a toothpick test. Remove once it comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

Applying frosting too soon

Why it happens Frosting melts on warm bars, making a mess and reducing visual appeal.

Fix Allow bars to cool completely at room temperature before spreading frosting evenly.

Variations

Add chopped nuts

Stir in 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans to the batter for a crunchy texture contrast. Nuts add richness and a toasty flavor that pairs well with the spices and cream cheese frosting.

Note: Fold nuts in gently at the end to avoid breaking them up and to keep the batter evenly textured.

Use whole wheat flour

Swap half or all of the all-purpose flour with whole wheat flour for a nuttier flavor and added fiber. The bars will be slightly denser but still moist and delicious.

Note: Whole wheat flour absorbs more liquid, so you may need to reduce the amount slightly or add an extra egg for moisture.

Spice it up

Add 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg or ginger to the dry ingredients for a warmer spice profile. This variation adds depth and complexity, perfect for fall or winter baking.

Note: Increase spices cautiously to avoid overpowering the delicate zucchini flavor.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

Store zucchini bars in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days. Keep away from direct sunlight or heat to prevent frosting from melting.

Refrigerator

Keep bars refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 5 days. The frosting firms up and the bars remain moist. Bring to room temperature before serving for best flavor.

Freezer

Freeze bars wrapped tightly in plastic wrap and foil for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then bring to room temperature before serving. Frosting texture may soften slightly after freezing.

Reheating

Reheat bars gently in a low oven (300°F) for 10-15 minutes if desired. Avoid microwave reheating as it can melt the frosting unevenly. Add a splash of milk to the bars if they feel dry.

Make Ahead

Prepare bars up to 2 days ahead and refrigerate unfrosted. Frost just before serving to keep the frosting fresh and creamy.

FAQ

Can I use frozen zucchini for these bars?
Yes, but be sure to thaw and squeeze out all excess moisture very well. Frozen zucchini tends to release more water, so extra squeezing is crucial to avoid soggy bars.
Can I make these bars gluten-free?
You can substitute the flour with a gluten-free all-purpose blend, but the texture may be slightly different. Make sure your blend contains xanthan gum for structure.
How long do the bars stay fresh?
Stored properly in the fridge, the bars stay fresh for up to 5 days. The flavor often improves after a day as the spices meld together.
Can I skip the cream cheese frosting?
Yes, but the frosting adds moisture and tang that complements the bars. Without it, the bars are still tasty but less rich.
Can I add other vegetables instead of zucchini?
Grated carrots can work well as a substitute, adding sweetness and moisture. Avoid watery vegetables like cucumber without proper draining.
Why do my bars sometimes turn out dry?
Dry bars usually result from overbaking or not enough moisture in the batter. Make sure to check baking time carefully and drain zucchini properly to keep moisture balanced.
How do I store leftover bars with frosting?
Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator. The frosting will firm up and the bars will stay moist. Bring to room temperature before serving for best taste.
