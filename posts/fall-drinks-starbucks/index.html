If you love the cozy vibes of autumn, these Starbucks fall drinks bring that warmth right to your cup. From pumpkin spice to caramel apple, each sip feels like a hug on a chilly day. Perfect for weekend mornings or a quick afternoon pick-me-up!

Introduction

I’ve always been a sucker for those warm, comforting drinks Starbucks rolls out every fall. The moment pumpkin spice season hits, I’m all in. But I realized making these drinks at home is not only easier than I thought, it also lets me tweak the flavors just how my family likes them. This version blends fresh pumpkin puree with a cozy mix of spices and a touch of brown sugar, giving you that classic Starbucks vibe without the line or extra cost. The trick I learned? Heating the milk gently with the spices to really bring out the aroma before adding coffee. It makes the drink feel extra indulgent and rich, just like the real deal. Plus, topping it off with whipped cream and caramel drizzle is the perfect finishing touch to make it feel special. Whether you’re craving a quiet morning treat or a sweet afternoon pick-me-up, this recipe feels like a warm hug in a cup every time.

What You'll Need

Approximately 2 servings

  • 2 cups whole milk or milk alternative
  • 2 tablespoons pumpkin puree
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 2 shots espresso or 1/2 cup strong brewed coffee
  • Whipped cream for topping
  • Caramel sauce for drizzle

How To Do It

Step 1
In a small saucepan, combine the milk, pumpkin puree, brown sugar, vanilla extract, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cloves. Warm the mixture over medium heat, whisking frequently to blend the spices and prevent the milk from scorching. Heating the milk slowly allows the spices to infuse fully, creating a rich, aromatic base that’s key to the drink’s flavor. Avoid boiling, as that can change the texture and taste of the milk. You want it hot but smooth, around 160°F if you have a thermometer.
⏱ Time: 7 minutes
✓ Doneness: Milk mixture is steaming with visible spice specks and a smooth, frothy surface
Step 2
While the milk mixture warms, prepare your espresso shots or strong brewed coffee. Pour the coffee into your cups, then slowly add the hot pumpkin milk mixture, stirring gently to combine. This layering helps keep the flavors distinct yet harmonious. If you’re rushing, the spices won’t have as much time to bloom, and the drink may taste flat. The coffee’s bitterness balances the sweet and spiced milk perfectly.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Drink is steaming hot with a creamy, light orange hue and a well-blended aroma
Step 3
Top each drink generously with whipped cream and drizzle caramel sauce over the top. This final touch adds a luscious texture and a sweet finish that’s signature to Starbucks fall drinks. You can even sprinkle a pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg on top for extra warmth. The contrast between the creamy whipped topping and the spiced drink below is what makes this treat so irresistible.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Whipped cream holds its shape with a glossy caramel drizzle and a light dusting of spice
Step 4
Serve immediately while hot for the best experience. If you want to make it extra special, use a frother to create silky foam on top of the milk before adding it to the coffee. This mimics the café texture and makes each sip feel luxurious. Enjoy your homemade Starbucks fall drink with a cozy blanket or your favorite book!
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Drink is hot with a smooth, foamy top and inviting aroma
Step 5
For a caramel apple version, add 1/4 cup unsweetened apple juice to the milk mixture before heating. This adds a fresh fruit note that pairs beautifully with the warm spices. Stir well and adjust sweetness as needed. It’s a lovely twist that feels like autumn in a cup.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Milk mixture has a light amber color and a sweet, fruity aroma
Step 6
If you prefer a dairy-free option, swap the whole milk for almond, oat, or coconut milk. These alternatives add their own unique flavors and textures, so choose one you enjoy. Heat gently as with regular milk to avoid curdling and maintain smoothness.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Milk alternative is hot and well-blended with spices, no separation visible
Step 7
To make this drink extra creamy, stir in 2 tablespoons of heavy cream or coconut cream right before serving. This enriches the texture and adds indulgence, perfect for special occasions or when you want to treat yourself.
⏱ Time: 1 minute
✓ Doneness: Drink has a thicker, silkier consistency and a glossy appearance
Step 8
For a caffeine-free version, use decaf espresso or substitute coffee with strong brewed rooibos tea. This keeps the warm, spiced flavor without the buzz, great for evenings or sensitive drinkers.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Drink steams gently with a rich aroma but no bitter coffee notes
Step 9
Clean your saucepan and whisk promptly after use to prevent pumpkin and spice residue from sticking and hardening. This keeps your kitchen tools in good shape and ready for your next cozy drink session.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Pan and whisk are spotless and residue-free

Pro Tips

Warm the milk slowly over medium heat, whisking constantly.
Heating milk slowly prevents scorching and allows the spices to infuse evenly, creating a smooth and flavorful base. Rushing this step can cause burnt milk flavor or uneven spice distribution.
Use fresh pumpkin puree instead of canned pie filling.
Pure pumpkin puree has a natural, earthy flavor without added sugars or spices, giving you control over sweetness and spice levels. Pie filling has pre-added ingredients that can overpower or unbalance the drink.
Top with whipped cream and caramel drizzle right before serving.
Adding toppings last preserves their texture and visual appeal, enhancing the indulgent experience. If added too early, whipped cream can melt and lose its fluffy texture.
Use a milk frother for a silky foam finish.
Frothing the milk creates a creamy texture that mimics Starbucks drinks, adding a luxurious mouthfeel and making the drink more visually appealing.
Adjust spice levels to your taste by starting with less and adding more.
Spices can be potent, and personal preferences vary. Gradually adding spices helps avoid overpowering the drink and allows you to find the perfect balance.
Serve immediately while hot for the best flavor and texture.
The drink tastes freshest and the whipped cream topping holds best when served right away. Delays can cause separation or loss of aroma.
Store leftover pumpkin spice milk mixture separately from coffee.
Keeping components separate preserves freshness and allows you to reheat without overcooking the coffee, which can become bitter.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Boiling the milk mixture

Why it happens Many cooks rush heating and let the milk boil, thinking it will speed up the process.

Fix Heat the milk gently over medium heat, stirring constantly. Stop as soon as steam rises and small bubbles form around the edges. Boiling can cause the milk to curdle and develop a burnt flavor.

Using pumpkin pie filling instead of puree

Why it happens Pumpkin pie filling is convenient and looks similar to puree, so it’s an easy mix-up.

Fix Always check labels and use pure pumpkin puree to control sugar and spice levels. Pie filling contains added sugar and spices that alter the intended flavor.

Adding coffee too early

Why it happens Some add coffee to the milk while heating, thinking it will blend better.

Fix Prepare coffee separately and add it after the milk mixture is heated to preserve coffee’s bold flavor and avoid bitterness.

Skipping the spice infusion step

Why it happens In a hurry, cooks sometimes add spices directly to coffee or skip warming the milk with spices.

Fix Warm the milk with spices first to allow full flavor extraction and a smooth, integrated taste.

Not whisking the milk mixture

Why it happens Some assume heating alone is enough without stirring.

Fix Whisk frequently to prevent lumps, ensure even spice distribution, and create a creamy texture.

Variations

Caramel Apple Fall Drink

Add 1/4 cup unsweetened apple juice to the milk and pumpkin mixture before heating. This adds a fresh, fruity note that pairs beautifully with the warm spices and caramel topping, creating a delightful twist on the classic pumpkin spice.

Note: Heating the apple juice with the spices helps meld flavors but be careful not to boil to preserve the juice’s brightness.

Dairy-Free Pumpkin Spice Latte

Swap whole milk for oat or almond milk and use coconut whipped cream for topping. This version keeps the creaminess and spice but is perfect for those avoiding dairy without sacrificing flavor.

Note: Heat plant-based milks gently as they can curdle if overheated; avoid boiling.

Extra Creamy Pumpkin Latte

Stir in 2 tablespoons heavy cream or coconut cream after heating the milk mixture for a richer texture. This makes the drink feel extra indulgent and luxurious, ideal for special occasions.

Note: Add cream off heat to maintain its texture and avoid separation.

Decaf Fall Spice Latte

Use decaffeinated espresso or strong brewed rooibos tea instead of coffee. This caffeine-free option lets you enjoy the cozy flavors any time of day without the buzz.

Note: Rooibos tea should be brewed strong to mimic coffee’s boldness.

Spiced Mocha Pumpkin Latte

Add 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder to the milk and pumpkin mixture before heating for a chocolatey twist. This variation combines the best of fall spices and rich chocolate for a decadent treat.

Note: Whisk cocoa powder well to avoid clumps and adjust sugar to balance bitterness.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

The pumpkin spice milk mixture can safely sit at room temperature for up to 2 hours if covered, but it’s best to refrigerate promptly to maintain freshness.

Refrigerator

Store leftover milk mixture in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Coffee should be stored separately. Reheat gently on the stove or microwave before combining. Flavor may mellow slightly when cold.

Freezer

Freeze the milk mixture in a sealed container for up to 1 month. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat gently. Avoid freezing coffee as it can become bitter and watery when thawed.

Reheating

Reheat the milk mixture over low heat, stirring frequently to prevent scorching. Add a splash of fresh milk if it thickens too much. Avoid boiling to preserve texture and flavor.

Make Ahead

Prepare the spiced pumpkin milk mixture 1-2 days ahead and refrigerate. Brew fresh coffee and assemble drinks just before serving for the best flavor and texture.

FAQ

Can I use canned pumpkin pie filling instead of pumpkin puree?
It’s best to avoid canned pumpkin pie filling because it contains added sugar and spices that can alter the flavor balance of your drink. Pure pumpkin puree gives you control over sweetness and spice levels, resulting in a fresher, more authentic taste.
What’s the best milk to use for this recipe?
Whole milk provides the creamiest texture and best flavor, but you can use oat or almond milk for dairy-free options. Just heat plant-based milks gently to avoid curdling. Avoid soy milk as it may separate when heated with coffee.
How do I make this drink less sweet?
You can reduce or omit the brown sugar to lower sweetness. Keep in mind the sugar balances the spices and pumpkin’s earthiness, so taste as you go to maintain a pleasant flavor profile.
Can I make this drink ahead of time?
Yes, you can prepare the spiced pumpkin milk mixture up to 2 days in advance and refrigerate it. Brew fresh coffee when ready to serve and combine just before drinking to retain the best flavor and texture.
How do I froth milk without a frother?
You can whisk the hot milk vigorously by hand or shake it in a tightly sealed jar to create foam. While it won’t be as silky as a frother, it adds nice texture and mimics the café experience.
Can I use instant coffee instead of espresso?
Yes, dissolve 1-2 teaspoons of instant espresso powder in hot water for a quick substitute. It won’t have the same depth as fresh espresso but works well in a pinch.
What spices can I omit if I don’t have them all?
Cinnamon and nutmeg are the most essential for that classic fall flavor. You can skip cloves or ginger if unavailable, but the drink will be less complex. Adjust spice quantities to your preference.
Is there a caffeine-free version of this drink?
Absolutely! Use decaf espresso or substitute coffee with strong brewed rooibos tea for a caffeine-free option that still delivers warm, spiced flavors.
