These cozy fall drinks inspired by Dutch Bros are just what you need to warm up chilly autumn afternoons. With rich caramel and cinnamon flavors and a creamy whipped topping, they’re a perfect treat to sip while enjoying the crisp air.

Introduction

I first discovered these fall drinks at Dutch Bros a few years ago, and they quickly became my go-to treat as the leaves started turning. There’s something about the blend of warm spices, caramel sweetness, and creamy whipped topping that just screams autumn comfort. Over time, I tweaked the recipe to get that perfect balance of flavors and creaminess at home without needing to run out to the coffee shop. What makes this version stand out is the use of freshly brewed strong coffee combined with steamed milk and a homemade caramel-cinnamon blend that’s just right—not too sweet, but perfectly cozy. I also love adding a touch of nutmeg for that extra warm spice note. If you’re like me and love sipping something rich and soothing on a crisp fall afternoon, this recipe will feel like a warm hug in a mug. Plus, it’s super simple to make and perfect for sharing with a friend or two. I promise once you try it, you’ll want to make it a seasonal staple too!

What You'll Need

2 servings

  • 1 cup strong brewed coffee, hot
  • 1/2 cup steamed milk
  • 2 tablespoons caramel sauce, plus extra for drizzle
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • Whipped cream for topping
  • Cinnamon sticks for garnish (optional)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by brewing a strong cup of coffee using your preferred method. The strength is important because it balances the sweetness of the caramel and spices. If your coffee is too weak, the drink will taste flat. Aim for a rich, robust brew that still has a smooth finish. While the coffee brews, warm your milk in a small saucepan or using a milk steamer until hot but not boiling. Warm milk helps blend the flavors and creates that creamy texture you want in this drink.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Coffee is freshly brewed and steaming; milk is hot to the touch but not scalding.
Step 2
In a separate small bowl, mix together the caramel sauce, ground cinnamon, nutmeg, and brown sugar until well combined. This spice-caramel blend is the heart of the flavor, so take your time to get it smooth. Pour the hot coffee into two mugs, then stir in the caramel-spice mixture thoroughly so it dissolves completely. Pour the steamed milk over the coffee mixture, stirring gently to combine. This layering results in a warm, balanced drink with a luscious texture.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: The caramel and spices are fully dissolved; the drink has a smooth, even color with no graininess.
Step 3
Top each drink generously with whipped cream. This adds a light, creamy contrast to the warm spiced coffee and makes the drink feel indulgent. For an extra touch, drizzle caramel sauce over the whipped cream and sprinkle a pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg on top. Garnish with a cinnamon stick if you like; it adds a lovely aroma and makes the drink look inviting. Serve immediately to enjoy the contrast of hot coffee and cool whipped cream.
⏱ Time: 2 minutes
✓ Doneness: Whipped cream is fluffy and holds shape; caramel drizzle glistens on top.

Pro Tips

Always use freshly brewed coffee for best flavor.
Coffee flavor degrades quickly after brewing due to oxidation and cooling. Fresh coffee ensures a robust, aromatic base that balances the sweetness and spices. Using stale or reheated coffee results in a flat, bitter drink.
Steam milk gently to avoid scalding.
Overheated milk develops a burnt taste and loses sweetness. Steaming to just below boiling preserves milk’s natural sugars and creates a creamy texture that blends smoothly with coffee and caramel.
Mix caramel and spices thoroughly before adding to coffee.
Ensuring the caramel and spices are fully combined prevents graininess and uneven flavor distribution. This step helps dissolve sugar crystals and infuses the spices evenly, enhancing the drink’s consistency.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using weak coffee that gets lost in sweetness.

Why it happens Many people use mild coffee thinking it’s less bitter, but it ends up tasting flat and overly sweet when combined with caramel and spices.

Fix Brew a strong, fresh coffee using a medium to dark roast. This balances the sweet and spice notes and prevents the drink from tasting one-dimensional.

Overheating milk until it boils.

Why it happens Rushing the milk steaming step or not monitoring temperature causes milk to scorch, ruining flavor and texture.

Fix Heat milk gently to around 150°F, just below boiling. Use a thermometer or steam wand to control temperature for a smooth, sweet milk base.

Variations

Pumpkin Spice Dutch Bros Fall Drink

Add 1 tablespoon pumpkin puree and a pinch of pumpkin pie spice to the caramel-spice mixture for a seasonal twist. This adds a creamy, earthy flavor that pairs beautifully with the cinnamon and nutmeg.

Note: Pumpkin puree adds moisture, so reduce milk slightly to keep drink balanced.

Vegan Dutch Bros Fall Drink

Use oat milk or almond milk steamed gently instead of dairy milk, and swap whipped cream for coconut whipped topping. This keeps the creamy texture and warm spices intact while being dairy-free.

Note: Plant-based milk can foam differently; steam carefully to avoid separating.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This drink is best enjoyed immediately. If left at room temperature, consume within 30 minutes to avoid loss of flavor and texture. Cover loosely to prevent dust or contaminants.

Refrigerator

Store leftover coffee and milk mixture in an airtight container for up to 24 hours. Whipped cream topping is best added fresh. Reheat gently on the stove or microwave before serving, stirring to recombine.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended due to texture changes in milk and whipped cream. Caramel sauce can be frozen separately for up to 3 months.

Reheating

Reheat the coffee and milk mixture gently on the stovetop over low heat or in short bursts in the microwave. Avoid boiling to prevent milk curdling. Add fresh whipped cream and caramel drizzle after reheating.

Make Ahead

Prepare the caramel-spice mix and brewed coffee in advance and refrigerate separately. Steam milk and assemble the drink just before serving for the best texture and flavor.

FAQ

Can I use instant coffee for this recipe?
Instant coffee generally lacks the depth and richness needed for this drink, resulting in a flat flavor. For best results, brew fresh coffee using a drip machine, French press, or espresso maker to get a robust base that balances the sweetness and spices.
What’s the best way to steam milk at home without a machine?
You can heat milk gently on the stove until hot but not boiling, then whisk vigorously to create foam. Alternatively, pour hot milk into a jar, seal it tightly, and shake vigorously until frothy. This won’t be as smooth as a steam wand, but it works well in a pinch.
Can I make this drink iced?
Absolutely! Brew the coffee strong and chill it before mixing with cold milk and the caramel-spice blend. Serve over ice and top with whipped cream and caramel drizzle for a refreshing fall-inspired iced coffee.
How can I make this drink less sweet?
Reduce the caramel sauce amount or omit the brown sugar. You can also use unsweetened milk and skip the whipped cream topping. Adjusting these elements lets you control sweetness without losing the warm spice character.
Is there a dairy-free option for the whipped cream?
Yes, coconut whipped cream or almond milk-based whipped toppings work great as dairy-free alternatives. They provide similar creaminess and complement the fall spices nicely.
Can I prepare the drink ahead of time for a party?
Prepare the coffee and caramel-spice mixture in advance and keep refrigerated. Steam milk and assemble drinks just before serving to maintain the best texture and temperature. Whipped cream topping should be added last-minute.
What’s the shelf life of the homemade caramel sauce?
Store homemade caramel sauce in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to two weeks. Warm gently before use to restore its pourable consistency.
Can I add other spices like cloves or allspice?
Yes, a pinch of cloves or allspice can add complexity, but be cautious as these spices are potent. Start with a small amount to avoid overpowering the drink’s balanced flavor.
