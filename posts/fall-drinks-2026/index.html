This iced pumpkin spice latte is my go-to for cozy fall vibes without the fuss. It’s creamy, lightly spiced, and perfect for sipping on crisp afternoons. The secret? Fresh cinnamon dusting that wakes up every sip!

Introduction

I first made this iced pumpkin spice latte a few years ago when I wanted all the cozy fall flavors without the long wait at coffee shops. It quickly became a family favorite, especially on crisp autumn afternoons when the air turns chilly but the sun still shines bright. What sets my version apart is the fresh pumpkin puree combined with just the right blend of spices and a touch of maple syrup for natural sweetness. I also use cold brewed coffee to keep it smooth and less bitter, which makes the drink incredibly balanced and refreshing. One little trick I learned is to mix the pumpkin and spices with the milk before adding it to the coffee; this helps the flavors blend seamlessly and gives the latte a creamy texture that feels indulgent but still light. The final result is a beautifully layered iced drink with a rich, velvety mouthfeel and that unmistakable warm spice aroma. It’s perfect for anyone who loves fall flavors but wants something easy and homemade. Plus, it’s quick to whip up and looks so pretty in a glass topped with cinnamon dusting. I think you’ll love how comforting and satisfying this iced pumpkin spice latte is—whether you’re sipping it on your porch or sharing it with friends.

What You'll Need

2 servings

  • 1 cup cold brewed coffee
  • 1/2 cup milk (whole or your favorite alternative)
  • 2 tablespoons pumpkin puree
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Ice cubes
  • Ground cinnamon for dusting
  • Optional: whipped cream for topping

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by brewing a strong batch of coffee, ideally cold brewed to reduce bitterness and enhance smoothness. If you don’t have cold brew, brew your favorite coffee and chill it in the fridge for at least an hour. The coffee’s strength is key here because it balances the sweetness and creaminess of the pumpkin milk mixture. Pour 1 cup of cold coffee into a large glass filled halfway with ice cubes to keep the drink perfectly chilled without diluting it too fast.
⏱ Time: 10
✓ Doneness: Coffee should be cold and robust in flavor, ice cubes solid and not melting too quickly.
Step 2
In a separate bowl or jar, whisk together the milk, pumpkin puree, maple syrup, pumpkin pie spice, and vanilla extract until smooth and creamy. This step is crucial because it blends the spices evenly and creates a luscious texture. If you rush this or skip whisking thoroughly, you’ll end up with clumps of pumpkin or uneven spice flavor in the latte.
⏱ Time: 5
✓ Doneness: Mixture should be smooth, creamy, and homogenous without lumps, with a pleasant pumpkin aroma.
Step 3
Slowly pour the pumpkin milk mixture over the iced coffee to create a beautiful layered effect. The contrast between the dark coffee and creamy pumpkin milk looks inviting and feels luxurious. Stir gently if you prefer a blended latte or leave as is for a layered presentation.
⏱ Time: 2
✓ Doneness: Layers distinct but able to be mixed easily; creamy top visible.
Step 4
Dust the top with ground cinnamon for that warm, spicy aroma and a pretty finishing touch. This final step adds a burst of fresh spice with every sip and makes the drink feel extra special. You can also add a cinnamon stick as garnish for a rustic look.
⏱ Time: 1
✓ Doneness: Cinnamon powder evenly dusted, no clumps, aroma noticeable.
Step 5
Optional: Add a dollop of whipped cream on top for a richer, more indulgent drink. This works especially well if you’re serving it as a treat or dessert beverage. The whipped cream melts slightly into the cinnamon dusting adding a creamy sweetness.
⏱ Time: 1
✓ Doneness: Whipped cream fluffy and fresh, melting slightly into the cinnamon.
Step 6
Serve immediately with a straw or a spoon to enjoy the layers. The cold temperature and creamy texture combined with the warming spices make this perfect for a crisp fall day or any time you want a comforting iced drink.
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Drink cold and refreshing, spices aromatic.
Step 7
If making ahead, prepare the pumpkin milk mixture and coffee separately and combine just before serving to keep the ice from melting too quickly and diluting the flavors.
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Components fresh and cold, combined at serving time.
Step 8
Adjust sweetness to taste by adding more or less maple syrup. Some prefer it sweeter, others more balanced with the coffee bitterness, so taste as you go.
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Sweetness balanced to personal preference.
Step 9
For a vegan version, substitute dairy milk with almond, oat, or soy milk, and use maple syrup or agave for sweetness. The pumpkin flavor and spices still shine through beautifully.
⏱ Time: 0
✓ Doneness: Smooth creamy texture, no separation.

Pro Tips

Use cold brewed coffee for a smoother, less bitter base.
Cold brew coffee is brewed slowly with cold water, extracting fewer bitter compounds and acids. This results in a smoother, mellower flavor that pairs better with sweet and creamy ingredients. Using hot brewed coffee chilled quickly can sometimes taste harsher or more acidic.
Whisk pumpkin puree with milk before adding to coffee.
Whisking emulsifies the pumpkin and spices into the milk, preventing clumps and ensuring an even flavor and creamy texture. If you add pumpkin directly to coffee without mixing, it can separate or settle unevenly.
Dust cinnamon powder fresh just before serving.
Cinnamon powder releases its aroma best when freshly sprinkled. Adding it too early causes it to absorb moisture and lose its fragrance. Fresh dusting also creates a visually appealing topping that enhances the drinking experience.
Serve immediately over ice to maintain texture and flavor.
Ice keeps the latte chilled and refreshing but melts over time, diluting the drink. Serving immediately ensures you enjoy the full flavor and creamy texture before the ice melts significantly.
Adjust sweetness with maple syrup to taste.
Everyone’s sweetness preference varies, so tasting and adjusting maple syrup lets you customize the drink. Maple syrup also adds a subtle flavor depth that refined sugars lack.
Use a tall clear glass to showcase the layered look.
The visual appeal of the layered coffee and pumpkin milk mixture is part of the latte’s charm. A tall clear glass shows off the contrast and makes the drink feel more special and inviting.
Try different milk alternatives to find your favorite flavor.
Different milk types impact flavor and texture. Oat milk adds natural sweetness and creaminess, almond milk is lighter with a nutty note, and soy milk is neutral and creamy. Experiment to find what you like best.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Using pumpkin pie filling instead of pure pumpkin puree.

Why it happens Many assume pumpkin pie filling is interchangeable with pumpkin puree, but it contains added sugars and spices that can overpower the latte and make it too sweet or cloying.

Fix Always use pure pumpkin puree without additives. This lets you control the sweetness and spice levels precisely, resulting in a balanced and fresh-tasting latte.

Not whisking the pumpkin mixture thoroughly.

Why it happens Rushing the mixing step can leave lumps of pumpkin or uneven spice distribution, causing inconsistent texture and flavor in the latte.

Fix Take time to whisk the pumpkin, spices, and milk until completely smooth and homogenous. This ensures every sip tastes perfect and creamy.

Using hot coffee poured directly over ice.

Why it happens Pouring hot coffee over ice melts the ice rapidly, diluting the drink and weakening the flavor.

Fix Use cold brewed coffee or chill hot brewed coffee in the fridge before assembling the latte. This keeps the drink cold without watering it down.

Adding cinnamon powder too early.

Why it happens Adding cinnamon powder before serving causes it to absorb moisture and lose aroma and flavor.

Fix Dust cinnamon powder fresh just before serving to preserve its fragrance and create a visually appealing top.

Skipping the vanilla extract.

Why it happens Some skip vanilla thinking it’s optional, but it adds a subtle sweetness and depth that rounds out the spices and pumpkin flavor.

Fix Always include vanilla extract in the pumpkin milk mixture for a more complex and balanced flavor.

Variations

Vegan Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte

Swap dairy milk for oat, almond, or soy milk and use maple syrup or agave for sweetness to make this latte completely vegan. The spices and pumpkin shine through just as well, and oat milk adds a natural creaminess that works beautifully.

Note: Whisk plant-based milk thoroughly with pumpkin and spices to prevent separation. Adjust sweetness as some plant milks are naturally sweeter.

Spiced Pumpkin Chai Latte

Add 1/2 teaspoon of chai spice blend or brewed chai tea concentrate to the pumpkin milk mixture for an extra layer of warming spices like cardamom and ginger. This creates a chai-pumpkin fusion that’s cozy and aromatic.

Note: Reduce pumpkin pie spice slightly to avoid overpowering the chai spices. Stir gently to combine flavors.

Pumpkin Spice Latte with Espresso Shot

For a stronger coffee kick, add a shot of hot espresso to the cold brew before layering with pumpkin milk. This amps up the caffeine and adds a rich coffee flavor that pairs well with the spices.

Note: Ensure espresso is cooled slightly before mixing with cold brew to avoid melting ice too fast.

Sugar-Free Pumpkin Spice Latte

Skip the maple syrup and use a sugar-free sweetener like stevia or monk fruit. This keeps the latte low in sugar while still delivering the cozy pumpkin spice flavor.

Note: Adjust the sweetener amount carefully as sugar substitutes vary in sweetness intensity.

Pumpkin Spice Latte Smoothie

Blend the pumpkin milk mixture with ice cubes and a frozen banana for a creamy, smoothie-style pumpkin spice drink. It’s thicker and perfect for a fall breakfast or snack.

Note: Use less ice if you want a thicker smoothie and add a splash of milk to help blending.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This iced latte is best enjoyed immediately. If left at room temperature, it should be consumed within 1 hour to avoid spoilage and loss of texture. Cover loosely if needed to prevent dust.

Refrigerator

Store the pumpkin milk mixture and brewed coffee separately in airtight containers in the fridge for up to 2 days. When ready to serve, combine over fresh ice. The flavor remains fresh but the ice must be added fresh for best texture.

Freezer

Freezing is not recommended as the pumpkin milk mixture may separate and the texture suffers. Coffee can be frozen in ice cube trays for future iced drinks.

Reheating

This drink is intended to be served cold. If you want a warm version, gently heat the pumpkin milk mixture on the stove until warm and mix with hot coffee, skipping ice. Do not microwave assembled iced latte as it will separate.

Make Ahead

Prepare the pumpkin milk mixture and brew coffee up to 1 day ahead and store separately in the fridge. Assemble with ice just before serving to keep the drink fresh and chilled.

FAQ

Can I use canned pumpkin pie filling instead of pumpkin puree?
I don’t recommend it because pumpkin pie filling contains added sugars and spices that can throw off the flavor balance of the latte. Using pure pumpkin puree gives you control over sweetness and spice levels, resulting in a fresher and more balanced drink.
What’s the best milk to use for this latte?
Whole milk gives the creamiest texture and richest flavor, but any milk or milk alternative works. Oat milk is a popular choice for its natural sweetness and creaminess. Almond and soy milk are lighter but still tasty. Just whisk well to combine with the pumpkin.
How do I make this latte less sweet?
Simply reduce or omit the maple syrup. The pumpkin and spices add natural flavor, so you can adjust sweetness to your taste. If you want a sugar-free option, use a low-carb sweetener like stevia or monk fruit.
Can I make this latte hot instead of iced?
Yes! Heat the pumpkin milk mixture gently on the stove and combine with hot brewed coffee instead of cold brew. Skip the ice and cinnamon dusting for a warm, comforting pumpkin spice latte.
How do I prevent the pumpkin from clumping in the milk?
Whisk the pumpkin puree thoroughly with the milk and spices before adding to the coffee. This emulsifies the mixture and prevents clumps, ensuring a smooth and creamy texture.
Can I prepare this latte in advance?
You can prepare the pumpkin milk mixture and coffee separately and store them in the fridge for up to 2 days. Combine over fresh ice just before serving to keep the drink fresh and cold.
What if I don’t have pumpkin pie spice?
You can make your own by mixing cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cloves in equal parts. This blend replicates the warm, aromatic flavor of pumpkin pie spice and works perfectly in the latte.
Can I add espresso instead of coffee?
Absolutely! Adding a shot of espresso gives a stronger coffee flavor and more caffeine kick. Just cool the espresso slightly before mixing with cold brew and pumpkin milk to avoid melting the ice too fast.

Final Thoughts
